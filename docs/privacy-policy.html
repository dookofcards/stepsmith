Stepsmith Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

The short version

Stepsmith runs entirely on your device. The guides and screenshots you record are stored in your browser and are never sent anywhere. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no ads and no accounts. We operate no servers.

The free version makes no network requests. If you choose to buy Stepsmith Pro, the purchase is handled by ExtensionPay (which uses Stripe). That is the only situation in which Stepsmith contacts a server. It happens only after you click the unlock button, and it never sends your guides, screenshots, captions or anything about your browsing.

What Stepsmith records, and only while you record

Recording starts when you click "Record this tab" and applies to that one tab until you stop or close it. While recording, Stepsmith saves:

  • a screenshot of the visible part of the recorded tab for each step;
  • the visible label of what you clicked (for example "Save") and the name of a field you typed into (for example "Email") to write each step's caption;
  • the page title and website name, to name the guide and its first step.

Stepsmith never saves what you type. It only notes that you typed in a field. It never reads the contents of password, one-time-code or card-detail fields, and it blurs those fields in screenshots automatically. Screenshots and labels do show whatever is on the page, such as names or numbers, so review a guide before you share it. Pro adds a redaction tool and automatic blurring of email addresses and long numbers in text fields; to do that, a page script checks whether a field's text looks like an email address or a long number and reports only the position of the field, never its text.

Where it is stored

Guides, captions and screenshots are stored in your browser's local database (IndexedDB) for the extension, on your device. Your Pro status and branding choices (accent colour, footer text, logo) are stored in the extension's local storage on your device. The state of a recording in progress is kept in memory-only session storage and disappears when the browser closes. Stepsmith does not use Chrome sync storage, so none of this is synced to other devices by Stepsmith. You can delete every guide and screenshot at any time with "Delete all my guides and screenshots" in Settings, or by removing the extension.

What Stepsmith accesses, and why

  • Storage: keeps your Pro status and branding on your device, and the state of a recording in progress.
  • Scripting: injects the recorder into the tab you are recording, and only into that tab.
  • Access to websites (optional): Stepsmith asks for this once, with Chrome's own prompt, the first time you start a recording. It is needed to keep recording after a click opens a new page and to take the screenshots. It is used only on the tab you are recording while you record. Nothing is transmitted. If you decline, nothing is recorded.
  • extensionpay.com: a small script runs on extensionpay.com pages only, so that the extension learns a purchase completed.

Buying Pro

Clicking the unlock button opens ExtensionPay's checkout in a new tab. Your payment details and email address are entered on ExtensionPay's and Stripe's pages, not in Stepsmith, and are governed by their policies. After you have started a purchase, Stepsmith may check your Pro status with ExtensionPay about once a week. This contains no information about your guides or browsing. If you never click the unlock button, Stepsmith never contacts ExtensionPay.

Children, changes and contact

Stepsmith does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. If this policy changes, the effective date above will change with it. Questions: github.com/dookofcards/stepsmith/issues.
