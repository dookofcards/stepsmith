# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-24

First release.

- Record the steps you take in a tab, across page loads. Each click becomes
  a step with a screenshot, the clicked spot highlighted, and an automatic
  caption such as "Click **Save**".
- Typing becomes "Type in **Email**". What you type is never saved. Password,
  one-time-code and card fields are never read and are blurred in screenshots.
- Edit captions, reorder, delete and add text-only steps. Unlimited guides,
  stored on your device.
- Export a single self-contained HTML file, or copy the steps as Markdown.
- Pro (one-time unlock): PDF export, redact and annotate screenshots (blur,
  black out, box, arrow, text, crop), automatic blurring of emails and long
  numbers, your own logo, colour and footer on exports, and Markdown export as
  a .zip with image files.
- The free version makes no network requests.
